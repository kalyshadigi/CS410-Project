## AI Changelog
>For major project work involving substantial AI assistance, teams should maintain a concise AI
>Engineering Log documenting meaningful uses of AI. The log should focus on important engineering
>interactions rather than recording every prompt. Entries should identify, when appropriate:
>- what problem the team was trying to solve;
>- why AI was used;
>- what the AI recommended;
>- whether the recommendation was accepted, modified, or rejected;
>- why the team made that decision; and
>- how the result was verified.
>  
>Students should not assume that submitting a transcript of AI prompts is a substitute for engineering
reasoning
